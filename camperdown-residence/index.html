The residence

Considered spaces. Timeless details.

This project reflects a modern approach to urban residential design, balancing practical family living with an understated material palette.

Open-plan spaces, bespoke joinery, generous natural light and carefully composed interior finishes create a cohesive experience across the home.

Project overview

Built around the way people live.

The photographs document the home's external architecture and a range of interior spaces, including living areas, kitchen, bedrooms, bathrooms and integrated storage.

A restrained palette of timber, stone and light-toned finishes connects these spaces, while thoughtful openings and transitions help bring daylight into the home.
