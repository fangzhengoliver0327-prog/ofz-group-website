About Us.

Outstanding. Functional. Zenith.

OFZ Pty Ltd is a licensed builder based in Sydney, NSW, operating under the OFZ Group name. We provide building and construction services across residential and commercial projects, with a focus on quality workmanship, clear communication and practical solutions.

Our scope includes new homes and duplexes, renovations and extensions, commercial fit-outs, waterproofing and facility improvement works. We approach every project with careful planning, reliable trade coordination and attention to detail, from the initial discussion through to completion.

As a licensed builder, we understand the importance of building requirements, site safety and accountability. Our goal is to make the construction process straightforward and transparent, while delivering spaces that are functional, durable and built with intention.

Our Services.

Licensed building and construction services across Sydney, with a focus on quality workmanship, reliable trade coordination and practical project delivery.

New Homes & Duplexes

Construction of new homes and duplexes, including site works, structural works, roofing, internal fit-out, finishing trades and handover coordination.

Renovations & Extensions

Home renovations, additions and extensions, including structural alterations, kitchen and bathroom upgrades, and integration with existing buildings.

Commercial Fit-outs

Shop, office and commercial fit-outs, refurbishments, partitions, ceilings, flooring and coordination of building services and finishing trades.

Building improvements, refurbishments and maintenance-related construction works for schools, childcare centres and other facilities.
