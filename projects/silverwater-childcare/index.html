Project Experience / Education & Childcare

Silverwater
Childcare Centre.

A purpose-built early learning environment in Silverwater, Sydney. This new construction project brings together spaces for arrival, learning, infant care and everyday childcare operations.

The project

Designed around everyday learning.

The centre brings together a distinctive exterior, an accessible approach to the entrance and a series of interior environments tailored to the day-to-day needs of early childhood education.

From the welcoming reception area to learning rooms and child-sized amenities, the spaces demonstrate the range of considerations involved in a new-build childcare facility.

LocationSilverwater, Sydney NSW
SectorEducation & Childcare
Project typeNew Construction
Project overview

Spaces with a clear purpose.

The photographs document several key areas of the completed building: its street-facing exterior, reception, a learning space, children’s bathroom facilities and an infant care room.

Across these areas, practical circulation, age-appropriate facilities and varied finishes are important parts of the overall environment. The project provides relevant experience in the delivery of a specialised education and childcare building.

This project is included as professional experience gained in a previous role, rather than as a project contracted or delivered by OFZ Group.
